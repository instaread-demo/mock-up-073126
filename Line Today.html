First Street Project Timeline

July 27, 2026 Tony Vigliotti Local News

(Havre) – Senior Engineering Project Manager for the Montana Department of Transportation Mike Moore told New Media Broadcasters what the timeline looks like for the completion of the US-2/First Street project in Havre.

“We are on track, if not a little ahead. The public can look forward to seeing the crosswalk work and striping operation start maybe the first week of August. We’ll be doing sign work here later this week and we’re hoping to wrap the whole thing up probably early September right before Festival Days.”

You can find the latest updates of the US-2 pavement preservation project by visiting the Montana Department of Transportation Website.
