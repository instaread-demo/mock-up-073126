Indoor Rock Climbing Gyms for Kids in Long Beach & Orange County – Southern California

Updated: July 2026

Indoor climbing gyms are one of the best-kept secrets for active kids. They build strength, problem-solving skills, and confidence, all while kids think they’re just having a blast scaling colorful walls.

The good news: you don’t have to drive far. The Greater Long Beach and Orange County area have several great options, from beginner-friendly bouldering gyms to massive facilities with zip lines, aerial silks, and 50-foot walls.

Featured

Ultimate Ninjas

5462 E. La Palma Ave., Anaheim
(714) 795-1970

Play on obstacle and agility courses, including a four-level warped wall, salmon ladder, fly wheels, flying squirrel, jumping spider, quintuple steps, and more.

Open play in one and two-hour sessions, plus birthday parties, seasonal camps during most school holidays, competitions, and weekly classes. Kids classes for ages 3 and up run beginner through advanced levels, building strength, endurance, and confidence.

First class is free for a monthly membership. No sign-up fee, no long-term commitment. Sibling discounts available.

  • View Website

Aesthetic Climbing Gym

26794 Vista Terrace, Lake Forest

A family-friendly environment for climbers of all levels. Youth programs include Little Rockstars for toddlers, climbing teams, homeschool programs, and camps. Rock climbing birthday parties also offered.

  • View Website

Hapik Climbing Center

El Paseo Shopping Center
8712 Garfield Ave., South Gate
(562) 268-2100

Climb your way through a colorful indoor adventure with themed walls, vertical challenges, and ropes courses reaching up to 26 feet high. With over 100 climbing routes across 30 unique walls, there’s something for all skill levels to explore. An auto-belay system allows kids to climb independently while staying safely secured. Birthday party packages are available, making it a fun and active destination for groups.

  • View Website

Long Beach Rising

205 E. Anaheim St., Long Beach
(562) 612-3008

A bouldering climbing gym and fitness facility welcoming all ages and skill levels. Youth training, competition, and recreation teams are available for kids who want to take their climbing further. Special events and birthday parties can also be booked here.

  • View Website

Rockreation Climbing Center

1300 Logan Ave., Costa Mesa
11866 La Grange Ave. Los Angeles

Climb indoor walls designed for all skill levels, from beginners learning rope and belay basics to more advanced climbers building technique and strength. Families can enjoy open climb sessions and dedicated kids/parent climb times in a supportive environment. Ongoing programs like Rock Warriors offer weekly training for ages 6–18. Camps and birthday party packages are also available.

  • View Website

Sender One Climbing

4949 Lakewood Blvd., Lakewood
1441 S. Village Way, Santa Ana
26505 Aliso Creek Road, Aliso Viejo

Indoor climbing center featuring 50-foot walls for roped climbing and bouldering, plus Sender City, a unique interactive climbing area for every age. Each location includes challenges like Spider Web, Trembling Towers, Drop Slide, Fun Walls, Jump Catch, and Mineshaft (climbing in the dark!).

Youth programs include drop-in classes, camps, Climbing Academy, and climbing teams for kids. Party packages also available.

Additional locations in Los Angeles, Playa Vista, and Westwood.

  • View Website

More Rock Climbing Centers:

  • Hangar 18 – Long Beach, Mission Viejo, Orange, San Clemente, South Bay & More
  • Movement – Fountain Valley
  • Class 5 – Torrance

Indoor climbing is a great way to keep kids active year-round, rain or shine. Most of these gyms offer day passes, so it’s easy to try one out before committing to a membership or program. Check each gym’s website for current hours, pricing, and any upcoming events or specials.
