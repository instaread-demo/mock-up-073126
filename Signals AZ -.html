City of Prescott Plans Downtown Rainwater Harvesting Improvements

The City of Prescott is inviting residents, business owners, and visitors to learn more about the proposed Montezuma Rainwater Harvesting Project during a public meeting on Thursday, July 30. Community members will have the opportunity to meet with Environmental Project Managers, ask questions, and share feedback on the proposed improvements.

The project would add landscaped rainwater basins along Highway 89 near 302 S. Montezuma Street. These basins are designed to capture and naturally filter stormwater before it reaches Granite Creek, helping improve water quality, support native vegetation, and enhance the appearance of downtown Prescott.

Public Meeting Details

The public meeting will take place:

  • Date: Thursday, July 30, 2026
  • Time: 4:30 p.m. to 6:30 p.m.
  • Location: Air Village Grass Lawn | 302 S. Montezuma Street, Prescott

Community Feedback Encouraged

City officials encourage anyone who lives, works, or travels through the area to attend the meeting and provide input. The project is intended to improve stormwater management while creating a more attractive streetscape and supporting the health of Granite Creek through natural water filtration and native landscaping.

Residents are invited to stop by during the meeting to learn more about the proposed project and share their thoughts before plans move forward.
