SECU MD Launches Partnership with Dulaney Valley Memorial Gardens

July 27, 2026

Partnership provides affordable memorial planning options to help Maryland families prepare for the future

Listen to audio version of this article
Sponsored by
0:00 / 3:36
Instaread

SECU, Maryland’s largest state-chartered credit union, is proud to announce a new partnership with Dulaney Valley Memorial Gardens. This partnership will provide SECU members with exclusive savings and financing options for cemetery planning, helping families prepare for one of life’s most important decisions with greater peace of mind.

SECU members will receive special member-only pricing and financing on arrangements at Dulaney Valley Memorial Gardens and a complimentary Personal Planning Guide designed to help families understand their options and make informed decisions.

By partnering with Dulaney Valley Memorial Gardens, SECU is expanding its commitment to supporting members through every stage of life with valuable and trusted local resources. Despite being one of the most difficult conversations to initiate, planning for end-of-life arrangements is one of the most thoughtful gifts individuals can give their loved ones.

“At SECU, we’re passionate about helping our members navigate every major life milestone with care and confidence– not just their financial goals, but the important decisions that bring peace of mind for themselves and their families,” said Nora Bamberg, director of business development at SECU. “We’re proud to partner with Dulaney Valley Memorial Gardens to provide our members with meaningful benefits that make planning ahead more affordable and accessible.”

For nearly 70 years, Dulaney Valley Memorial Gardens, located in Timonium, Md., has served Baltimore-area families with compassionate guidance and personalized care. As a family-owned cemetery, the organization has helped generations of families plan or navigate the loss of a loved one with dignity and respect.

“Dulaney Valley Memorial Gardens has offered compassionate and personalized service to more than 35,000 families since opening in 1958. We are passionate about serving our client families as they plan for the future. It is also our honor to serve families who have suffered a loss,” said Mitch Rose, general manager of Dulaney Valley Memorial Gardens. “We are excited about our new partnership with SECU because we will have the opportunity to serve its members and help them achieve the peace of mind that comes with having all of their final arrangements complete.”

SECU members participating in the program receive:

  • Exclusive member-only pricing and financing on cemetery arrangements

  • A complimentary Personal Planning Guide

  • Personalized assistance from Dulaney Valley Memorial Gardens’ experienced planning team

About SECU (State Employees’ Credit Union of Maryland)

SECU (“see-Q”), Maryland’s largest state-chartered credit union, serves over 250,000 members via 25 financial centers across the state, including 18 full-service centers, four digital centers, and a Virtual Financial Center. As a member-owned, not-for-profit organization, SECU puts its members first. Membership is open to all of Maryland, and with over 1,900 ways to join, you may be eligible through a family member, work, the college you attended, state employment, an organization you are affiliated with, or simply through a one-time donation to the SECU MD Foundation. Headquartered in Linthicum, Md., SECU offers convenient access to over 50,000 free ATMs through the CO-OP network, as well as access to expert financial planners. With more than $5.5 billion in assets, SECU ranks among the top 60 credit unions nationwide based on total assets in the U.S. Insured by NCUA. For more information, visit www.secumd.org or follow SECU on Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, and TikTok.

About Dulaney Valley Memorial Gardens

For nearly 70 years, Dulaney Valley Memorial Gardens has served Baltimore-area families with compassionate cemetery and memorial planning services. As a family-owned cemetery, Dulaney Valley Memorial Gardens is committed to providing personalized guidance and care, helping families honor loved ones while planning for the future with confidence and peace of mind. To learn more, visit dulaneyvalley.com.
