Melons & Mahjong Brings a Taste of Summer to ROW DTLA

  • Written By: Lorenzo Dela Rama
  • July 21, 2026

Fresh fruit and friendly competition share the table at Melons & Mahjong, a summer gathering hosted by LA Mahjong League and Weiser Family Farms. Taking place Thursday, July 23, at ROW DTLA, the event pairs a curated melon tasting and beverages with 90 minutes of Taiwanese 16-tile mahjong.

Guests can select from three experience levels: beginners receive guided instruction, intermediate players sharpen their strategy and learn LA Mahjong League house rules, and experienced players can join an open-play session—with a fresh melon waiting for the winner.

Beyond the game, the afternoon celebrates local agriculture, food access and community. For every ticket sold, $5 will be donated to Food Forward, a Los Angeles nonprofit working to reduce food waste and fight hunger.

For more information about the event and to buy tickets, lamahjongleague.com

Event Details:

When: Thursday, July 23; 4:30–6 p.m. or 6:30–8 p.m.
Where: ROW DTLA, 777 S. Alameda St., Los Angeles
Tickets: From $35; advance reservations required
