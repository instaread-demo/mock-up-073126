Nottoway Sheriff Robert Jones said at deadline that foul play is not suspected in the death of a man discovered Monday morning (July 20th) in the 700 block of Hungarytown Road west of Blackstone. Jones said deputies were dispatched at 6:08 a.m. for a report of a “subject lying beside the roadway and not moving.” Jones said that upon arrival, deputies discovered a deceased white male, appearing to be between ages 25-35, and believed to be affiliated with FASTC. Several dozen FASTC trainees were staying Sunday night at the nearby Inn at Blackstone. Jones said the body was taken to the State Medical Examiner’s Office in Richmond and that efforts were still being made to notify next-of-kin before releasing the man’s identity. Lt. Ty Moore is the investigating officer. One source told the Courier- Record that there were no obvious signs that the man was struck by a vehicle and that it’s likely he suffered a medical emergency while running or walking, possibly Sunday evening.
