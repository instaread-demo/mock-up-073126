5 Must-Watch Movies This Summer

5 Must-Watch Movies This Summer

Logan Scully

Listen to audio version of this article
Sponsored by
0:00 / 4:23
Instaread

The past two months have been a rollercoaster for the movie industry. Films like Backrooms and Obsession have taken over the theaters, with earnings surpassing higher budget features like The Mandalorian and Grogu. Box office expectations vs reality have been all over the place.

Here we discuss our five favorite movies for the months of May and June, providing context while leaving out any spoilers. If you are looking for a next watch, keep reading to get insight on some of the most popular movies that were recently released.

Backrooms

From viral YouTube videos to cinemas worldwide, Novato High School graduate Kane Parsons has successfully blown away all expectations for his recent movie Backrooms. With the budget being set at only $10 million, the film has surpassed $350 million as of early July since its release on May 29, 2026; $118 million of this came from the opening weekend alone. The story begins when protagonist and furniture store owner Clark discovers a hidden passage to an alternate dimension in the store’s basement. After recruiting two of his younger employees to help record what they deem as “the backrooms,” chaos ensues as something lurks in the depths of the dimension. If you are looking for a trippy horror film, the Backrooms is for you.

Toy Story 5

Toy Story 5 has been one of the most highly anticipated films this summer, and continues the story of Bonnie, the current owner of the Toy Story toys. In this edition, the toys are faced with a new obstacle: technology. After struggling to make friends, Bonnie welcomes a new device called Lilypad, which results in the toys being kicked aside. Jessie, the leader of the crew, believes that she can do a better job than Lilypad at helping Bonnie make friends and teams up with Buzz Lightyear and Bullseye to do so. Whether you grew up with the series or are a current fan, Toy Story 5 provides everyone with relevant life lessons while staying comedic.

Star Wars: The Mandalorian and Grogu

Originating in 2019, The Mandalorian has taken over the Star Wars world, following the journey of Din Djarin (The Mandalorian) and his apprentice Grogu (Yoda lookalike) throughout the galaxy. After three seasons, the show went dark and began filming the follow-up movie which was just released on May 22, 2026. In the film, Djarin and Grogu attack imperial remnants while working for the New Republic, but run into trouble after they strike a deal with the powerful Hutt twins. Despite the film earning over $320 million worldwide at the time of writing this, it still must bring in over $200 million more to break even, making it the lowest-grossing Star Wars movie of all time. However, if you enjoyed the show and are a Star Wars enthusiast, the action-packed film will provide you with an enjoyable two hour and 12 minute experience. 

Obsession

Although Obsession technically premiered at the 2025 Toronto International Film Festival, the film was only released in theaters on May 15, 2026. Director Curry Barker began his film career on YouTube, uploading low-budget horror projects online, before gaining attention from producer James Harris. Harris loved one of Barker’s shorts called The Chair, and reached out hoping to change it into a feature film. Instead, Barker pitched the idea of Obsession which he claimed originated from The Simpsons episode Treehouse of Horror. Once accepted, the movie began shooting on a $750,000 budget, telling the story of Baron ‘Bear’ Bailey, a young man with a big crush on his childhood friend Nikki Freeman. After using an item called the ‘One Wish Willow’ to make Freeman fall in love with him, Bailey’s wish takes a turn for the worse and leads to his dream becoming a psychological nightmare. Grossing over $400 million worldwide, Obsession is a must watch for older teens and young adults who love romance-driven horror films.

Supergirl

Over 41 years after the original release of Supergirl, the new edition follows the life of Kara Zor-El and Krypto the Superdog, who, unlike her cousin Kal-El (Superman), grew up on a fragmented piece of their old planet, Krypton. This traumatic experience has greatly shaped her life and brought her to an alien planet on her birthday, where she meets a troubled alien girl named Ruthye Marye Knoll. Together, they work to track down antagonist Krem of the Yellow Hills, who has history with both of them.
