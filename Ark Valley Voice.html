Chaffee County Fair Tuesday to Feature Trail Horse Competition, 4-H Rabbit and Poultry Check-In, and Ranch Team Sort

Posted by Ark Valley Voice Staff | Jul 27, 2026

Jason LaRue shows off one of the Reserve Grand Champion rabbits during the 2024 Junior Livestock Auction. Photo by Carly Winchell.

The Chaffee County Fair and Rodeo continues with events each day this week. The fair concludes on Sunday, August 2.

Cash Courson shows off one of his three Reserve Grand Champion Freedom Ranger chickens at the 2024 Junior Livestock Auction. Photo by Carly Winchell.

Tuesday will begin with the Open Trail Horse Competition in the morning.

During the late afternoon, 4-H students will check-in their poultry and rabbits. The poultry and rabbit shows will follow on Wednesday, July 29.

The day will conclude with the Ranch Team Sort in the evening.

Schedule for Tuesday, July 28

9:00 a.m. Open Trail Horse Obstacle Competition

4:00 p.m. to 6:00 p.m. 4-H Rabbit and 4-H Poultry Check-In

6:00 p.m. Ranch Team Sort

Shannon Juilleret and her horse Magnum navigate the rope gate obstacle. Photo: Jim Cannella

The 2026 Fair Guide and more information about the fair and rodeo is available at ChaffeeCountyFair.com.
