Flau’jae Johnson Returns To Savannah For The 4th Annual Back To School Giveback

By Savannah Tribune | on July 27, 2026

Savannah’s own Flau’jae Johnson is coming home. On Sunday, July 26, 2026, the Seattle Storm guard, NCAA champion, chart-climbing musician, and community leader will host the fourth annual Back to School Giveback at Enmarket Arena, 620 Stiles Avenue, Savannah, GA 31415, from 2:00 PM to 5:00 PM, presented by her nonprofit foundation, More To 4 – The Flau’jae Johnson Foundation.

The free, family-friendly celebration allows Savannah students to get prepped for the school year ahead, with free backpacks, school supplies, shoes, uniforms, along with free braiding and haircut services. There will also be music, a live DJ, free food, games, and community resources. Last year’s Giveback drew more than 2,500 attendees to Enmarket Arena, and this year’s event is set to be the largest yet.

This year’s Giveback is made possible with the support of national sponsors Experian, PUMA, e.l.f. Cosmetics, BODYARMOR, E93, and Houston Premier Realty, all partners who share Flau’jae’s commitment to investing in the next generation.

“Savannah made me. Every year I come back, it’s bigger than basketball and bigger than music; it’s about making sure our kids walk into school with everything they need and knowing somebody believes in them,” said Flau’jae Johnson. “Year four is going to be the biggest one yet.”

Founded by Johnson and inspired by her jersey number, More To 4 is dedicated to empowering underserved youth by creating access to opportunities in sports, education, and the arts. The Back to School Giveback is the foundation’s signature event, and has become a beloved summer tradition for families across the Savannah community.
