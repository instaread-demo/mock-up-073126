    $6M gift to support new Washougal librarySubscriber Exclusive

    ‘Extraordinary generosity of The Endeavour Fund’ advances project

    By Doug Flanagan
    July 23, 2026  8:05 am
    Government, News, Washougal
    Listen to audio version of this article
    Sponsored by
    0:00 / 0:45

    The Fort Vancouver Regional Library District and the FVRL Foundation have announced a $6 million gift from The Endeavour Fund to support the new Washougal Community Library, scheduled to break ground this winter.

    The gift represents the largest philanthropic investment in the foundation’s history, according to a news release.
