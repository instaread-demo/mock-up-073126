Northwest Cider Association Celebrates Executive Director Emily  Ritchie’s Legacy and Announces Leadership Transition 

by Sip Magazine | Jul 23, 2026

Listen to audio version of this article
Sponsored by
0:00 / 3:32
Instaread

PORTLAND, OR – After nearly 12 years of leadership, Emily Ritchie has decided to step down as  Executive Director of the Northwest Cider Association (NWCA), a decision driven by her  desire to spend more time with her young family. 

Since joining NWCA in 2015, Ritchie has helped guide the organization from formative  beginnings to where it stands today, as one of the most well funded and respected regional  beverage associations in the country. During her tenure, the association expanded its  membership and impact, secured more than $4.5 million in grants and sponsorships,  strengthened advocacy efforts and developed signature programs including Northwest Cider  Symposium and the Northwest Cider Cup, recognized as one of the industry’s most rigorous  and prestigious cider competitions in the Pacific Northwest. Grant funding supported research,  orchard and fermentation education, quality initiatives, market development and exploration of  international trade opportunities, helping elevate the reputation of Northwest cider and extend its  influence far beyond the region.  

Over the past 16 years, NWCA has grown alongside the cider industry into a leading regional  trade association serving cideries, orchardists, allied supplier partners and cider fans across  Washington, Oregon, Idaho, Montana and British Columbia. Today, the organization is  recognized as a trusted voice for cider, a valuable resource for cider education and marketing  and a model for regional collaboration.  

“Emily’s leadership has had a lasting impact on both our organization and the Northwest cider  community,” said Andrew Byers, President of the NWCA Board of Directors. “She leaves NWCA  strong, resilient and well positioned for the future. On behalf of the Board, we are deeply grateful  for her vision, dedication and more than a decade of service.”  

In 2024, Ritchie received the American Cider Association’s Significant Impact Award, one of the  industry’s highest honors, recognizing her lasting contributions to the cider community. She was  also honored with two SIP Magazine awards, reflecting excellence in communications and  leadership.  

The Board emphasized that NWCA enters this transition from a position of strength with a highly  qualified and dedicated staff, more than a year of operating reserves, multiple active grant funded initiatives, a clear strategic plan, established organizational systems, and strong  relationships with members, partners and funders. 

The Board has appointed Jana Daisy-Ensign as Interim Director starting the transition Aug 1.  Daisy-Ensign has been with NWCA for nearly seven years, including serving as Deputy Director.  She brings more than 20 years of experience in the craft beverage industry, including 12 years  in the Pacific Northwest cider industry and nearly a decade of nonprofit leadership expertise.  One of the world’s first Certified Pommeiliers, she serves as Competition Co-Director of the  Northwest Cider Cup and received NWCA’s sole Northwest Cider Leadership Award in 2019.  

Ritchie reflects on the value of the relationships and connections made as she prepares for her  next chapter. “Serving the Northwest Cider Association has been the honor of my professional  career,” Ritchie said. “I am incredibly proud of what this community has accomplished together  

and grateful to the members, partners, volunteers, staff, and Board who made it possible. I  leave knowing the organization is strong and well positioned for continued success.” 

About Northwest Cider Association  

For more than 15 years, the Northwest Cider Association (NWCA) has brought cideries and  cider lovers together to learn, experience and enjoy the Northwest cider culture.  Representing more than 300 cider professionals throughout Washington, Oregon, Idaho,  Montana and British Columbia, the group is dedicated to supporting, promoting and  growing its thriving industry. For more information, visit NWCider.com and follow @nwcider  on social media.
