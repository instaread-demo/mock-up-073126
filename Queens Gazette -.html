Movies On The Waterfront Returns!

By qgazette
on July 23, 2026

FacebookTwitterEmailMessengerCopy LinkMessageShare

Hunters Point Parks Conservancy (HPPC) is pleased to announce the return of our 12th annual Movies on the Waterfront series at Hunter’s Point South Park starting Saturday, July 25. HPPC will be hosting four movies this year, premiering with Ferris Bueller’s Day Off, which came out 40 years ago this summer; followed by the original Jurassic Park on August 22, sponsored by TF Cornerstone;  the animated How to Train Your Dragon on September 5, sponsored by Shibley Day Camp; and The Nightmare Before Christmas as part of our 4th Annual Halloween on the Waterfront celebration on October 31.

HPPC is again working with Rooftop Films to provide a state-of-the-art viewing experience in the park, featuring a 30’ inflatable screen with enhanced video projection equipment, allowing for remarkable color, clarity and sound and a fantastic experience for all moviegoers even when the oval is completely full of spectators.

Rob Basch, President of the Hunters Point Parks Conservancy, said, “Nothing says summer better than movies on the LIC Waterfront.  We are very happy to once again bring the community together for movies at the best theatre anywhere.  We look forward to seeing everyone starting this Saturday night.”

HPPC thanks TF Cornerstone and Shibley Day Camp for sponsoring this beloved community program for its eleventh year.

To ensure a safe and enjoyable experience for all guests, we kindly remind everyone that alcoholic beverages are not permitted into the park, nor can they be consumed outside of the designated picnic area located next to Ottomanelli’s. We also encourage all moviegoers to help us maintain the park’s cleanliness by cleaning up any food or beverages they bring along. Thank you for your cooperation and support as we strive to make this a memorable experience for everyone.

More information on the events and any updates due to weather can be found at www.hunterspointparks.org.
