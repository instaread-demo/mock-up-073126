La Famiglia’s Sena family invites everyone to celebrate their 50th anniversary with their special nostalgia menu. Enjoy some of the signature dishes that have defined La Famiglia for generations. (August, La Famiglia, 8 S. Front St., Philadelphia, Pa., lafamiglia.com)

“Rising Up: Rocky and the Making of Monuments” traces more than two millennia of artists’ engagement with boxing and celebrity. Ancient sculptures, 19th-century European works, and images from the golden age of boxing in the United States, together with contemporary art, reveal how fighters have been shaped as public figures. More recently, artists including Andy Warhol, Jean-Michel Basquiat, Glenn Ligon, Hank Willis Thomas, and Lisa Brice revisit this history through the lens of race, gender, and celebrity. These works illuminate what visitors project onto the Rocky statue: ideals of the underdog –perseverance, spirit, and grit – values shaped by the history of the sport and by lived struggle and aspiration. (Through Aug. 2, Philadelphia Museum of Art, 2600 Benjamin Franklin Parkway, Philadelphia, Pa., philamuseum.org)

The Philadelphia Chinese Lantern Festival illuminates Franklin Square with awe-inspiring, interactive, larger-than-life lanterns. Enjoy live performances, delicious food and drink, the Franklin Square Fountain Show, and Franklin Square attractions. (Through Aug. 16, Franklin Square, 200 N. Sixth St., Philadelphia, Pa., historicphiladelphia.org/events/philadelphia-chinese-lantern-festival)

Every Tuesday and Thursday enjoy free one-of-a-kind creative experiences in the Wildwoods’ neighborhood parks during Arts in the Parks. Locations and craft themes vary each week. Bring the family, a blanket, and a beach chair and get crafty! (Through Aug. 27, the Wildwoods, N.J., wildwoodsnj.com/event/arts-in-the-parks)

The Rehoboth Art League’s Outdoor Show returns to celebrate its 53rd year. More than 125 artists and artisans from around the country will display and sell their fine art and fine crafts amidst the Art League’s 3.5-acre wooded campus, just blocks from Rehoboth Avenue in coastal Delaware. The show is a juried event yielding exceptional work, and many of the artists actively demonstrate their creativity during the show. (Aug. 1-2, 8-9, Rehoboth Art League,12 Dodds Lane, Rehoboth Beach, Del., rehobothartleague.org)

Actress/creator/producer/writer Lisa Ann Walter brings her stand-up comedy tour to Helium Comedy Club. Known for her role as Melissa Schemmenti in “Abbott Elementary,” she’s also starred in the feature films “The Parent Trap,” “Bruce Almighty,” “Shall We Dance,” “War of the Worlds,” and “Killers.” Lisa has headlined all over the country, hosted dozens of A-list events, and published a best-selling comedic memoire/essay. (Aug. 2-4, Helium Comedy Club, 2031 Sansom St., Philadelphia Pa., philadelphia.heliumcomedy.com)

The Clifford Brown Jazz Festival is Wilmington’s tribute to a great man and his legacy. This year’s festival marks the 39th event and the 69th anniversary of his passing. This year will include live painting during performances. (Aug. 2-8, Rodney Square, 901 N. Market St., Wilmington, Del., cliffordbrownjazzfest.org)

Don’t miss The Best of Delaware, a lively event that celebrates excellence and supports worthy causes. Featuring winners from Delaware Today’s annual readers’ poll, dance and savor a variety of delicious food and drinks from this year’s winners. Easterseals Delaware & Maryland’s Eastern Shore is
this year’s beneficiary. (Aug. 6, Chase Center on the Riverfront, 815 Justison St., Wilmington, Del., delawaretoday.com/events/best)

Let’s Hang On, America’s preeminent Frankie Valli and The Four Seasons tribute group, will perform all of Valli’s mega hits including: “Sherry,” “Big Girls Don’t Cry,” “Walk Like A Man,” “Can’t Take My Eyes Off Of You,” “December of 1963” and “Who Loves You.” (Aug. 8, Broadway Theatre of Pitman, 43 S. Broadway, Pitman, N.J., thebroadwaytheatre.org)

Hammonton Comedy Night features Italian American comedian Joe Matarese from “America’s Got Talent.” His act is deeply rooted in his South Jersey/Philadelphia upbringing. He embraces his roots so heavily that he titled his latest stand-up run the “It’s In The Wooder” tour. His TV appearances include “The Late Show with David Letterman,” “Comedy Central Presents Joe Matarese,” and “Chelsea Lately,” to name a few. (Aug. 7-8, Eagle Theatre, 208 Vine St., Hammonton, N.J., eagletheatre.org)

The Mario Lanza Institute presents The Artistry of Joseph Schmidt Lecture/Presentation. Look back in celebration of the tragic life and career of the legendary diminutive tenor with the larger-than-life voice. Schmidt will be featured in exciting and beautiful performances of opera and song from several of his very popular films. RSVP at 347-452-7192 by Aug. 8. (Aug. 15, Mario Lanza Institute, 1214 Reed St., Philadelphia, Pa., mariolanzainstitute.org)

A Delaware 250 event, “Learn to Drink Like a Patriot” is a Colonial Cocktail Class where you’ll discover the beverages that fueled the Revolution and learn how to make them. Re-create a selection of historic punches that represent the assortment of flavors that accompanied our nation’s birth. Participants will work in groups to make three punches during the class and receive the recipes for each to make them at home. (Aug. 27, Greenbank Mills & Philips Farm, 500 Greenbank Road, Wilmington, Del., greenbankmills.com)
