Phoenix football opens at Brunswick Aug. 28

Look to improve on last season’s 2-8 record
By dconner@thenewsrecord.com
on July 27, 2026

Dennis Smith Sun Staff Writer
FacebookTwitterEmailMessengerCopy LinkMessageShare

Mecklenburg County High School first-year football head coach Kevin White and the Phoenix will open the regular season at Brunswick Aug. 28. (Dennis Smith photo)

With most VHSL teams opening football practice later this week, it is never too early to take a look at the upcoming schedule.

Mecklenburg County High School first-year head coach Kevin White will look for his Phoenix to improve on last year’s 2-8 record. Mecklenburg has a pair of scrimmages scheduled as they head to Appomattox on Aug. 14 for a 6 p.m. contest and then travel to Rustburg on Thursday, Aug. 20 for another 6 p.m. tilt.

Brunswick High School coach Darrell Owens also will look for his squad to improve on last year’s 2-9 mark. The Bulldogs will scrimmage at Cumberland Aug. 14 and at Westmoreland on Aug. 21.

The Phoenix will open their regular season at Brunswick with a 7 p.m. kickoff Aug. 28. Mecklenburg topped the Bulldogs 47-14 in the opener last season.

Mecklenburg will welcome Hopewell for its home opener on Thursday, Sept. 3 before entertaining Amherst on Sept. 11 and Greensville on Sept. 18. The Phoenix fell 16-14 at Hopewell last season, lost 19-0 to Amherst, and fell 14-13 to Greensville.

The Phoenix will open Piedmont District play at Bassett Sept. 25 before welcoming Halifax Oct. 2. Mecklenburg fell 28-16 to Bassett last season and 29-14 to Halifax.

Mecklenburg will travel to Magna Vista Oct. 9 and play at King William Oct. 15.

The Phoenix fell 68-20 to Magna Vista last year and 66-42 to King William.

The Phoenix will welcome GW-Danville Oct. 23 and close out the regular season, following an open date at Tunstall, Nov. 6. Mecklenburg fell 64-10 to GW last season and topped Tunstall 37-0 in the season finale.

After playing its season opener at home against Mecklenburg, Brunswick will travel to Essex Sept. 4 and travel to Lunenburg Thursday, Sept. 10. The Bulldogs fell 54-0 to Essex last season and 54-14 to Lunenburg.

The Bulldogs will travel to Nottoway Sept. 18 and open the Tri-Rivers District campaign at Surry Sept. 25.

Brunswick fell 24-7 to Nottoway last season and topped Surry 25-0.

Brunswick will entertain Windsor Oct. 2 and play at Southampton Oct. 9. The Dogs fell 13-7 against the Dukes last season and fell 49-14 against the Indians.

The Bulldogs will welcome Franklin for homecoming Oct. 16 and following an open date, travel to Sussex Oct. 30 and welcome Greensville for the regular season finale Nov. 6.

Brunswick topped the Broncos 24-22 last season, fell 28-8 to the Tigers, and 42-22 against the Eagles.
